\section*{Quick Reference: Most Important Cases for Thesis}
%%──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Quick Reference: Most Important Cases for Thesis}

\noindent For the specific angle of AI + competition law in Canada with EU/US comparison, the five most essential cases are:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{\textit{TREB} (Canada, 2017)} --- Data access as a competition law issue; the closest Canadian precedent to AI data dominance.
  \item \textbf{\textit{United States v.\ Google LLC} (Search, 2024)} --- First modern ruling that an AI-adjacent platform illegally maintained a search monopoly.
  \item \textbf{\textit{Bundeskartellamt v.\ Facebook} / \textit{Meta v.\ Bundeskartellamt} (CJEU C-252/21, 2023)} --- Landmark data-as-dominance case; privacy breach confirms market power.
  \item \textbf{\textit{Google Shopping} (EU, AT.39740, 2017)} --- Algorithmic self-preferencing as abuse of dominance; foundational EU digital case.
  \item \textbf{\textit{DOJ v.\ RealPage} (2024)} --- First major US enforcement action explicitly targeting AI-driven algorithmic pricing as collusion.
\end{enumerate}
